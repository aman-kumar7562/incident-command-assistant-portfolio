# Output Contracts

Adapt these structures to the user’s request and any approved organizational template. Do not present these examples as a company-approved format.

## Short current-state briefing

**Status:** Active / monitoring / reported restored / validated restored / unknown

**Customer impact:** What users could not do, known scope and figures, and what remains unknown.

**What changed:** Confirmed cause or leading hypothesis, plus the evidence boundary.

**Response:** Completed mitigation and validation; ongoing or planned work clearly separated.

**Next decision:** The one or two questions most likely to change the next action.

## Timeline

| Time (with timezone) | Event | Evidence | State |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Known timestamp or unknown | What happened | Source | Observed / reported / planned |

## Action review

| Action | Owner | State | Evidence / next checkpoint |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |

Do not create owners, dates, identifiers, or commitments that are not in evidence.

## Draft status update

Provide only the fields requested by the applicable template. Keep the language concise and customer-centered. Mark unresolved facts explicitly. End with:

> Draft for human review. Verify against current evidence and the organization’s approved incident communication guidance before sharing.
